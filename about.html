Our Story

EKKAB Store: Where Dreams Met Pixels and Baskets Blossomed In 2012, within a sun-drenched studio, three visionaries—Ella, Kieran, and Abigail—conjured EKKAB Store. Their initials became a canvas for an online marketplace where artisans’ creations danced with discerning shoppers. Pixels and code harmonized as Ella designed, Kieran coded, and Abigail wove stories. Each product—be it a Tuscan mug or a silver necklace—held a whispered backstory. Word spread, love letters bloomed in reviews, and EKKAB Store thrived—a symphony of makers, dreamers, and that antique coffee table where it all began.
